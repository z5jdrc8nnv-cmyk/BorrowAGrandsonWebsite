Why this exists

Not a service. A standing offer.

I visit my grandmother almost every day. Her building is full of people just like her — sharp and independent, but one loose hinge or confusing phone update away from needing a hand.

Borrow a Grandson is that same offer, open to her neighbors too. Call, email, or message me, pick a time, and I'll come by and do the kind of thing a grandson does. No fee, no catch.

What a grandson is for

If it's the kind of thing you'd never bother your own grandson over, that's exactly the kind of thing to call about.

Rearranging furniture, carrying boxes, lifting anything you shouldn't be lifting alone.

Small fixes around the place
